Enter through the person

Identity resolution comes before recollection.

A normalized alias is an identity handle—not proof that every attached claim is true. Resolve first, disambiguate compactly when needed, then retrieve person-scoped evidence using the live conversational need.

Progressive disclosure is relevance discipline

Retrieve enough to avoid amnesia. Reveal only what improves this exchange.

00

Silent readiness

Resolve once when the person first matters and keep a cue packet ready when surfacing it would only add noise.

01

Cue

Identity, why the person matters now, and zero to three current items—plus a light path to the brief or full history.

02

Brief

Relationship context, active threads, preferences, recent interaction, open loops, commitments, and material caveats that could affect the present decision.

03

Dossier

The governed record, timeline, aliases, sources, relations, contradictions, and supersession history—only when requested and within the chosen sensitivity boundary.

More stored data does not force a deeper response. A larger haystack is not automatically more useful, however confidently it accessorizes.

Dunbar circles help maintain relationships without scoring people.

The user sets or corrects the circle. Message volume, job title, social status, and data abundance never infer intimacy.

Evidence state governs confidence

Keep what was checked, witnessed, reported, inferred, missing, and disputed meaningfully different.

verified

Directly checked against an accountable source for the narrow claim.

observed

The user or an authorized tool directly witnessed the event.

reported

Someone supplied the claim without independent verification.

inferred

A bounded interpretation derived from named evidence and kept easy to retract.

unknown

The record explicitly acknowledges a missing answer instead of decorating the gap.

disputed

Material evidence or authority conflicts and the disagreement remains live.

recorded_atWhen Dunbar received the item.

effective_atWhen the item applied, if known.

expires_atWhen the item should stop guiding action without refresh.

Remember relationships without reducing people

Interaction texture beats personality labels.

USEFUL RECORD
“Prefers a short written brief before strategy calls.”
“Agreed to review the partnership draft next Tuesday.”
“In the July planning call, raised maintenance burden as the main objection.”
REDUCTIVE LABEL
“Difficult person.”
“Probably narcissistic.”
“Can be manipulated by urgency.”

Record situated behavior, context, source, and time. Never infer protected traits, diagnoses, motives, romantic interest, trustworthiness, or psychological scores.

Protect the person who is not in the room

Presence in the store never authorizes disclosure or action.

Minimize

Keep only details that support legitimate relationship continuity. A recognized name with an empty record is better than a richly upholstered hallucination.

Separate custody

The canonical SQLite record, rebuildable full-text index, retrieved evidence, and conversational synthesis remain distinct.

Exclude secrets

Credentials, recovery codes, government identifiers, financial account numbers, precise live location, and secrets stay outside Dunbar.

Require authority

Contact, publication, scraping, enrichment, third-party disclosure, and network sync require separate explicit permission.

Redact by default

Ambient resolution, cue, and brief paths exclude restricted fields. Restricted material belongs only in an explicitly requested dossier and suitable setting.

Keep claims challengeable

Corrections supersede rather than silently rewrite. Deterministic receipts prove the database transaction—not the underlying human claim.

A useful invocation

Prepare with context, not surveillance.

Use $dunbar to help me prepare for my conversation with Jordan. Resolve which Jordan I mean before retrieving anything, give me only the cue-level context that could materially improve this conversation, keep evidence states and open uncertainty visible, and offer the brief if deeper preparation would help.

For persistence, Dunbar uses stdin-based mutation commands for people, items, relations, and supersession. Resolve and recall currently place non-sensitive lookup text in command arguments, so those operations do not claim shell-history isolation.
